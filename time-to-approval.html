How Long Does Condo Offering Plan Approval Take?

The time from when a sponsor submits a New York City condominium offering plan to when the Attorney General accepts it for filing, across the 50 most recent plans accepted since Jan 9, 2026. The AG replaces a plan's submission date with each amendment's, so the original date is captured when a plan is first crawled and kept, and amended plans stay in the sample. Waits of two years or more are left out as likely record errors, so read the figures as a guide.
