The answer is in the plan.

Offering plans contain the details behind a development: what was offered, how the building was structured, and what changed along the way. Open Book helps you find those details across projects without opening every filing yourself.

How it works

  1. Search across projectsAsk a question or search by building, address, plan ID, or topic.
  2. See the evidenceResults show the matching language, the document it came from, and the page where you can check it.
  3. Follow the amendmentsSee when a later filing may have changed what the original plan said.

Coverage. Open Book searches the plans and amendments currently indexed here. The NY Attorney General has not posted documents for every filing, so a missing result does not mean a feature or project does not exist.

Source. New York State Attorney General, Real Estate Finance Bureau offering plan database. Open Book is an independent research tool and is not affiliated with the Attorney General’s office.

Plans describe what was offered at the time of filing. They do not establish what is currently available for sale. Check the original filing, relevant amendments, and current project information before relying on a result.
