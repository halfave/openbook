Coverage

What The Condo Book Project has read, and what it hasn't.

Counting…

Original Offering Plans Only

The Condo Book Project reads the original offering plan a sponsor filed with the New York State Attorney General. Later filings can change prices, unit counts, amenities, budgets and dates, and they are not read here. Check the Attorney General's record before relying on anything you find.

Full Text or Record Only

  • Full text searchable plans have their offering plan read page by page. They show as filled dots on the map, and their building pages have a search box.
  • Plan record only plans have the Attorney General's summary (name, address, unit and parking counts, dates, sponsor and counsel) but no text yet. The Attorney General has not posted documents for every filing, and some posted plans are scans that haven't been read yet.

A missing result does not mean a feature or project does not exist.

Extracted Details

For full-text plans, software reads the plan and pulls out the Schedule B budget and details such as the selling and managing agents, architect, working capital, reserve fund, parking arrangement, tax program and affordable housing. Each one shows the page it came from. They have not been checked by a person, so open the cited page before relying on one.

Sources

  • Offering plans and plan data: New York State Attorney General, Real Estate Finance Bureau offering plan database.
  • Building footprints used for the 3D massing images: NYC Building Footprints, NYC Open Data.
  • Building locations: NYC Department of City Planning GeoSearch.
