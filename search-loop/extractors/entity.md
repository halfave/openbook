# Extractor: entity → template `dossier`

## Search
- The entity's own site (about page, statute that creates it), then one independent source.

## Fields
`entity`, `kind`, `fields`: the 5–8 facts someone deciding something needs (what it does, who runs it / members, authority or legal basis, what it decides and when, latest decision or key number as a figure, contact/where records live). `sources`.

## Fallback
