- Zumper labels $7,200 'Average rent' in its table, but its methodology line says 'Median rent is calculated across all available listings'. Shown as average with the contradiction in the basis.

- The search snippet for RentReboot said $5,300 (July 2026, 10th–90th pct $3,400–$9,750); the fetched page says $5,500 for September 2026 with n = 1,862. Used the fetched page.
- Meraki Realty's $6,500 'average' cites Census ACS 2023 alongside its own data; not counted as current listing data.
- The metric template has one headline figure, but this question asks for three pivots (per unit, per bedroom, per sq ft). The other two went into 'derived'.
- RentReboot also publishes a 30th–70th percentile band for 2-bedrooms; it was dropped from the basis text because the render check only accepts dollar amounts that are figure objects, and the template has no slot for a percentile band.
