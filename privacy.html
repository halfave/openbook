Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

Open Book is operated by Half Ave Company LLC ("Half Ave", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Open Book website collects and what we do with it. The short version: no accounts, no cookies, no ad trackers, and we do not save your searches.

What we collect

Nothing you have to give us

You can use Open Book without an account, a name or an email address.

Your searches

When you search, your words and filters are sent to our database provider to run the search and return results. We do not store searches in our database or tie them to you.

Technical logs

Like any website, the services that host Open Book receive standard request information, such as your IP address, browser type, the time of the request and the address requested. These providers keep this information in short-term logs for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

Email you send us

If you write to us, for example to report an error, we keep your email address and message so we can reply and fix the problem.

Cookies and tracking

Open Book does not set cookies, does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and does not store anything in your browser.

Third parties the site connects to

  • Supabase, which hosts the search database.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces. Your browser requests fonts directly from Google, which receives your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Our web host, which serves the site's pages.

Links to the Attorney General's website, Google Maps Street View and other sites take you to those sites, whose own privacy policies apply.

How we use information

Only to run and secure the Service, fix problems, respond to you, and comply with law. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Public records

Open Book displays public filings. Some filings name sponsors, their principals, attorneys and other parties as part of the public record. If you believe something displayed here should not be, write to us and we will review it.

Children

Open Book is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect information we hold. Emails are kept as long as needed to handle the request. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by writing to hello@halfave.co.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
