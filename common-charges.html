What Are Common Charges in a New NYC Condo?

The monthly common charges set by the first-year budget (Schedule B) in 394 New York City condominium offering plans accepted since Sep 29, 2021. Buildings with commercial units are left out, so the budget falls on the homes (and any parking or storage units). Budgets are the sponsor's projections for the first year, not what owners pay today; real estate taxes are not included (see property taxes).
